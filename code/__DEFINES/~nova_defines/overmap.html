ZTRAIT_OVERMAPZ-trait flagging the dedicated overmap grid Z-level.
OVERMAP_DIMENSIONSWidth/height of the overmap grid (square).
MAX_OVERMAP_PLACEMENT_ATTEMPTSCap on placement retries when picking a free overmap tile.
OVERMAP_STAR_BUFFERMinimum chebyshev distance a placed POI must keep from the star at the center of the grid. The star sprite is drawn from overmap_large.dmi (96x96, with pixel_x = pixel_y = -32), so it visually occupies a 3x3 tile footprint centered on its own tile. A buffer of 2 keeps any 1x1 POI sprite just outside that footprint.
OVERMAP_CLUSTER_RUIN_MAX_SIDERuins with max(width, height) at or below this side length pack onto shared cluster Zs.
OVERMAP_CLUSTER_MIN_SEPARATIONMinimum chebyshev gap (tiles) between ruin footprints on a cluster Z.
OVERMAP_SITE_LZ_RUIN_SEPARATIONMin chebyshev gap between a landing zone and a ruin (LZs only need clearance, not the wide ruin-ruin debris spacing — that gap leaves dense clusters with 0 LZs).
OVERMAP_SITE_EDGE_MARGINEdge margin (tiles) from TRANSITIONEDGE when placing ruins / LZs on a site Z.
OVERMAP_SITE_LZ_SIDESide length of each seeded landing zone on a site Z.
OVERMAP_SITE_CAMERA_MARGINExtra tiles the astrogation camera may roam past an LZ bbox.
OVERMAP_SITE_PLACE_ATTEMPTSPlacement attempts before giving up on a ruin / LZ anchor.

OVERMAP_SCAN_COOLDOWNCooldown between active radar scans from a single ship.
OVERMAP_INTERACTION_RANGEChebyshev tile range for docking and direct overmap interactions.
OVERMAP_SCAN_DECAYTime before a scanned contact fades from the radar if not re-scanned.
OVERMAP_PHYSICS_WAITSSovermap global tick (events). Entity physics and orbits use SSfastprocess.wait (also 0.2s).
OVERMAP_SCREEN_UPDATE_INTERVALMinimum interval between throttled update_screen() rebuilds during flight (~20fps).
OVERMAP_INTERPOLATE_LIMITMax pixel displacement per physics tick. Prevents ships from clipping through edge turfs. Must be less than ICON_SIZE_ALL (32) to guarantee no tile-skipping. At 5Hz and the hard speed limit of 2, peak displacement is 12.8px/tick — comfortably under this cap.
OVERMAP_VELOCITY_EPSILONVelocity epsilon: below this threshold, consider the ship stopped. Must stay below one assisted hall/partial-throttle acceleration step.
OVERMAP_G0Gravitational constant for ISP/escape velocity calculations.
OVERMAP_MAX_SPEEDEmergency integration ceiling in tiles/second. Normal cruise is the lower flight-assisted envelope derived from thrust, mass, and braking distance.
OVERMAP_MANEUVERABILITYManeuverability: how quickly actual velocity converges on desired (0..1 per second). Retained for station-keeping / legacy autopilot; forward flight accel uses thrust/mass.
OVERMAP_THRUST_ACCEL_SCALEConverts thrust/mass into tiles/s² toward the throttle target. At 0.029, one healthy HNT (30 thrust) on a mass-100 ship crosses the 64×64 playable diagonal in roughly six minutes at full throttle.
OVERMAP_ASSIST_BRAKING_DISTANCEFlight computer target stopping distance in overmap tiles. The assisted speed envelope is sqrt(2 * available_acceleration * this distance).
OVERMAP_EMERGENCY_BRAKE_MULTIPLIEREmergency recovery brake authority relative to normal rated braking.
OVERMAP_EMERGENCY_BRAKE_HULL_DAMAGE_MINOne-shot hull integrity cost at minimum and maximum flight speed.
OVERMAP_EMERGENCY_BRAKE_ENGINE_DAMAGE_MINPhysical brute damage dealt to each active engine at minimum/maximum speed.
OVERMAP_FUEL_PLASMA_RATIODefault mapped fill mole fractions (plasma / oxygen).
OVERMAP_FUEL_DEFAULT_PRESSUREMapped shuttle chamber pressure target (~3 atm) at matter-bin T1 (servo setpoint).
OVERMAP_FUEL_PRESSURE_PER_BIN_TIERExtra operating pressure per matter-bin tier above T1.
OVERMAP_FUEL_RELIEF_RATEMax moles pushed chamber→L2 per tick when over servo pressure (intake stays gated).
OVERMAP_PROP_MOLES_PER_THRUSTThrust-to-moles knob; tune for ~10 min full-tank burn at full throttle.
FUEL_INJECTOR_ISP_NOMINAL_MAXNominal max ISP for thrust-efficiency display in the fuel injector UI.
OVERMAP_HNT_FEED_LAYERLayer-2 propellant manifold between fuel injectors and HNT engines.
OVERMAP_FEED_TRANSFER_RATEMax moles transferred chamber → L2 feed per atmos tick while idle (pressure-regulated). Kept well below typical chamber capacity (~9 mol at 3 atm) so idle ticks cannot empty the chamber.
OVERMAP_FEED_THRUST_TRANSFER_RATEMax moles chamber → L2 per atmos tick while engines want thrust (hot inventory turnover). Kept below ~half a T1 chamber so cold L1 refill cannot quench the fire in one tick.
OVERMAP_FEED_MIN_DELTA_PMinimum chamber-vs-feed pressure delta (kPa) before feed push runs.
OVERMAP_FEED_BUFFER_PRESSURETarget L2 feed pressure (kPa) to keep primed while idle; stop pushing once reached.
OVERMAP_FEED_MAX_CHAMBER_FRACTIONNever transfer more than this fraction of current chamber moles in one tick (idle).
OVERMAP_FEED_THRUST_CHAMBER_FRACTIONChamber fraction cap while thrusting — residence time for chemistry before refill.
OVERMAP_INLET_HEAT_MARGINInlet charge is heated at least this far above ignition while the chamber is lit.
OVERMAP_INLET_HEAT_POWER_MULTMultiplier on glow-plug wattage for heating L1 charge while ignited. Thrust refill (~3 mol/tick × ~130 J/mol/K × ~100 K) needs ~40 kJ/tick; base preheat alone (~10 kJ at 0.5 spt) was quenching the chamber.
OVERMAP_SPOOL_FULL_RAIL_PRESSUREL2 pressure (kPa) at which mass-flow spool-up is effectively instant.
OVERMAP_SPOOL_MIN_ACCELMinimum spool-up rate (mol/s²) even on a near-empty rail ("revving").
OVERMAP_SPOOL_DECELSpool-down rate (mol/s²); faster than spool-up so throttle cuts feel responsive.
OVERMAP_COLD_FLAMEOUT_TICKSAtmos ticks below ignition temperature before forced flameout (clears chamber_ignited).
OVERMAP_REACT_ITERATIONSMax LINDA react() iterations per burn tick.
OVERMAP_PREHEAT_POWER_BASEFuel injector glow-plug heater wattage per micro laser tier (heat delivered to the chamber).
OVERMAP_PREHEAT_SETPOINT_MAXHighest chamber preheat setpoint (K) reachable at a given micro laser rating.
OVERMAP_IGNITE_SPARK_ENERGYEnergy (joules) billed per igniter spark attempt, successful or not.
OVERMAP_THRUST_EPSILONThrottle below this is treated as "no thrust" for propellant draw.
OVERMAP_MOL_S_EPSILONMass-flow / spool rates below this are treated as zero.
SHIP_CONTROL_CONSOLEShip can be operated from a helm console (ByondUi camera + TGUI).
SHIP_CONTROL_DIRECTShip can be operated via NIF or neurohelm direct piloting.
