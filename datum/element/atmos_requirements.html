proc
check_safe_environment
(/mob/living/living_mob)

Don't call a corpse spawner's mob a mapping error.

A corpse spawner builds its mob alive and kills it on the line after the one that made it, so for that one line there is something breathing whatever the ruin has - vacuum, for the airless ones. Loading a template before SSair comes up hides that, because the mob then defers this check until the subsystem is ready and is long dead by the time it runs. Everything loaded afterwards, which is every overmap site and every dynamic encounter, is checked during the live line instead, and reports a stock space ruin as a mob mapped somewhere it cannot survive.

The spawner does not leave its tile until the mob it made is dead, so its presence is what tells the two apart.
