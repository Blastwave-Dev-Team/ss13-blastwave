overmap_radar_relay_does_not_bounce
/datum/unit_test/overmap_radar_relay_does_not_bounce

A processor and a bus wired to each other must not volley the same packet between them.

Each relays to the other's type, so with nothing tracking where a packet has been they pass it back and forth until BYOND aborts the sweep at a million stack frames. That took the whole ui_act() with it, which left the console holding contacts it had stored but never announced.

Asserting on the transcript because the console appends to it once per packet it accepts, so a packet that arrives more than once is visible without instrumenting the relay.
