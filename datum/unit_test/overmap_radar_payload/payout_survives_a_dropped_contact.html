payout_survives_a_dropped_contact
/datum/unit_test/overmap_radar_payload/payout_survives_a_dropped_contact

A contact that goes away mid-cycle costs a dot, not the console.

Whatever reopens the cycle cannot do so before the contact is already gone, so there is always a moment where the payout order names something no longer tracked. The interface stages its batches inside a state update, which means a null in the contact array is not a missing contact — it is an unhandled throw during render, and the operator gets a fatal exception in place of their radar.
