/datum/unit_test/overmap_radar_payload/drain_delivers_every_contact_once

Paying out in batches still has to deliver the whole picture, once.

The saving is worthless if it loses or duplicates contacts. A dropped contact is an operator not seeing a ship; a duplicated one would mean the interface is staging the same entry under two cycles and could retire it at the wrong moment.

Cursor advance is driven directly rather than through its timer, so this tests the payout logic without depending on timer subsystem scheduling inside a unit test.
