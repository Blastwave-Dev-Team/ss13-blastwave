hardlight_coverage
/datum/component/hardlight_coverage

Hard-light projection coverage

The bookkeeping half of a projector: which tiles it can hold a body on, and whether it is currently holding one. Deliberately knows nothing about charge, power, or combat, so that the machine can own all of that and this can be reused by anything else that projects a body onto a pad. Coverage is area-scoped rather than radial, matching how holopads decide where a hologram may stand, which is also what gives the encounter its "it cannot follow you out of the room" rule.
