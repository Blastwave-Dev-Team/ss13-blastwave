hardlight_recall
/datum/ai_planning_subtree/use_mob_ability/hardlight_recall

Snap back to the plate rather than be walked off it.

Fires when the body has been drawn away from its projector and whatever drew it away is not the most useful thing to be hitting. Without this the counterplay to the whole encounter is "one person jogs in circles at the far wall while everyone else works on the objective ten tiles away", because the body will dutifully chase them and the room is effectively cleared. With it, being led away is a thing the unit notices and undoes, and the person leaning on the plate is the one who pays for it.
