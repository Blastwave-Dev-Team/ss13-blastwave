priority_queue_entry
/datum/priority_queue_entry

Identity-keyed priority queue for AI targeting.

/datum/heap is a cmp-sorted pop pile. This is add/remove by the thing itself, then peek the highest rank that still exists. Encounters and other world-side owners hold one of these and hand the datum to a pawn via BB_PRIORITY_TARGET_QUEUE; the pawn does not own the entries.

Same target enqueued again updates its rank instead of duplicating.
