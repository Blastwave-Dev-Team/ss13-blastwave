Interdiction array board

Telecomms internals with a bluespace tap bolted on, which is what the salvaged arrays turn out to be once you get the hatch off them. The capacitor is the interesting slot: it alone decides how far the finished array reaches, so the tier-four units recovered from derelicts stay worth hauling home long after the tech itself is understood.
