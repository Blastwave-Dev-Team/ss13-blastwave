micro_reactor_on
/obj/effect/mapping_helpers/micro_reactor_on

Micro reactor activation helper

Drop on a reactor's tile and it comes up running. Reactors map in cold, which is correct for a derelict and wrong for anywhere the lights are meant to have been on since before the crew arrived; without this the only way to get one is a parallel /active subtype of every reactor variant, kept in step by hand.

Applies to any micro_reactor, so the B.A.P.G.M. is covered by the same helper.

Late because TogglePower() reaches for the soundloop the reactor builds in its own Initialize, and two atoms sharing a tile are initialised in whatever order the loader hands them over in.
