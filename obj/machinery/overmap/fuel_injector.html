proc
heat_intake_charge
(/datum/gas_mixture/charge, seconds_per_tick)

While lit, warm L1 charge to ignition (or chamber T up to laser max) before merge. Returns any moles that could not be heated to target (caller should return them to L1). Never merges partially-warmed cold gas — that was quenching under thrust.

proc
ignite_chamber
(/mob/user)

Spark the chamber. If the mix is combustible this kicks it to reaction temperature and lets the chemistry self-heat; if nothing reacts, the temperature bump is reverted so sparking can't be used as free heating.

linked_engines_want_thrust

proc
process_chamber_reaction
()

Keep a lit chamber burning: react every tick while chamber_ignited. Overpressure is an intake gate + L2 backpressure path (see process_feed_output), not a dump valve. Flameout when the mix stays cold below ignition.

proc
process_feed_output
()

Pressure-regulated push of chamber mix onto L2 (whole mix, unfiltered). Overpressure opens the rail even when idle — intake stays gated until the engines (or feed buffer) take enough mass that chamber P falls. No dump valve.

proc
process_preheat
(seconds_per_tick)

Glow-plug heating: walk the chamber toward the setpoint with bounded per-tick power. Heat delivered scales with micro laser tier; the grid is billed the delivered heat divided by preheat efficiency.

proc
process_tick_burn
(/list/obj/machinery/power/shuttle_engine/overmap/engines, moles_requested, dt)

Pull moles_requested from L2 and convert to thrust via ISP. dt scales rated thrust so moles_requested = mol_s * dt yields full thrust at full demand.
