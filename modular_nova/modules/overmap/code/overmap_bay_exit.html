OVERMAP_BAY_EXIT_DEPTHHow many tiles outward the per-column raycast probes before declaring a column clear.
/proc/overmap_bay_exit_ignoresTRUE if thing is planetary/decorative clutter that should not block launch paths.
/proc/overmap_bay_tile_is_wallTRUE if checked_turf is a hard wall that blocks a launch path. Closed turfs, closed blast doors, and anchored dense structures block; ordinary doors (openable by hand), open floors, and whitelisted decor (GLOB.overmap_bay_exit_ignore) do not.
/proc/overmap_bay_tile_is_outsideTRUE if reaching checked_turf means the raycast is definitively outside and can stop early. Only space qualifies: interior floor is something a ray must travel through, not a destination, or a sealed bay reads as open at depth 1. Planetary pads have no space turf nearby and instead pass by clearing the full depth budget.
