/proc/get_shipyard_routeNearest-ancestor route lookup for a mapped type.
/proc/get_shipyard_dedicated_pipe_fittingNearest-ancestor lookup for a fitting that dispenses this exact device.
/proc/get_shipyard_pipe_fittingThe fitting an RPD consumes to lay a mapped atmospherics device, which is also what the device deconstructs back into. Devices without a dedicated fitting bill the generic shape they are laid as.
/proc/shipyard_pipe_fitting_costMaterial content of the fitting an RPD consumes to lay a device.
/proc/get_shipyard_wallframeThe frame a mapped wall fixture is mounted from.
/proc/shipyard_part_item_typeThe physical item that satisfies a part requirement.
/proc/shipyard_dependency_designsDesigns whose output can satisfy one ship-plan dependency.
/proc/shipyard_dependency_designBaseline design embedded by a self-contained stock blueprint.
/proc/shipyard_dependency_design_ratingRelative quality of a dependency design's output.
/proc/shipyard_design_material_costSilo-storable material cost for a quantity of one selected design.
/proc/shipyard_dependency_material_costBaseline dependency cost used by static manifest analysis.
/proc/shipyard_printed_component_costSilo cost of printing a quantity of a loose machine component.
/datum/shipyard_routeDeclarative construction recipe for one mapped type and its descendants.
/datum/shipyard_route/turf_decalTurf decals are floor paint: applying one adds a decal to the turf and deletes the effect, so the printer reproduces the blueprint's markings for free instead of leaving the deck blank.
/datum/shipyard_route/docking_portRegistering the finished hull is what creates its docking port, so the mapped one is neither built nor missing and has nothing to report.
/datum/shipyard_route/structure_spawner/window/hollowHollow spawners choose their window set from the direction they were mapped in, which the manifest cannot reconstruct from the type alone.
/datum/shipyard_route/structureAnything structural the shipyard can price is generated atomically.
/datum/shipyard_route/greyscale_tableGreyscale furniture picks its stock when a crewmember crafts it, so the blueprint records no material for the shipyard to bill.
/datum/shipyard_route/machineryMachines split by whether they have a constructible board. A machine without one is not unbuildable, it is simply not built from a frame: wall equipment mounts from a frame item, so it is generated whole and priced from that stock instead.
/datum/shipyard_route/machinery/air_sensorWrenched down from a hand-held sensor, so the sensor is the whole cost.
/datum/shipyard_route/machinery/disposal_fixtureBins and chutes are welded from the same construct stock as the pipes they terminate, at the scale of a fixture rather than a segment.
/datum/shipyard_route/disposal_pipeDisposal pipes are welded from a single pipe segment's worth of plating.
/datum/shipyard_route/grilleA grille frames its window the way a girder frames a wall, so it goes down with the rest of the framing and is standing before anything is glazed.
/datum/shipyard_route/blast_doorPoddoors are fabricated from substantially more stock than ordinary airlocks.
/datum/shipyard_route/door_buttonDirectional door buttons share the ordinary button frame recipe.
