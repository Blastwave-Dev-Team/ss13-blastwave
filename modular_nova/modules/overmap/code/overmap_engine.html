/datum/gas_machine_connector/reversedA machine connector whose hidden L2 pipe port faces opposite the connected machine's facing. Thrusters point dir toward their exhaust, so their fuel intake (and the propellant manifold) sits on the back side. The stock connector always faces connected_machine.dir, which pointed the port at the exhaust tile and prevented pipes laid behind the engine from ever connecting.
