The choke point for every visible drop of blood this mob produces.

/mob/living/get_bloodtype() never reads species: it sends MOB_ROBOTIC straight to oil. Overriding here is what actually colors the flying splatter (temp_visual), the floor decal (make_blood_splatter) and blood left on weapons and clothing (get_blood_dna_list). The global robotic-to-oil fallback is left alone on purpose.
